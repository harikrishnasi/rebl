uild BrandSignup at src/pages/BrandSignup.jsx. Route: /brand/signup
3-step wizard with progress indicator at top.

STEP 1 — BRAND BASICS
  Brand name · Slug (auto-from-name, editable)
  Preview: 'Your drop page: {slug}.rebl.in'
  Email · Password · Short description (140 chars)
  Logo upload (circular preview)

STEP 2 — WHAT DO YOU SELL?
  Headline: 'What does your brand create?'
  Grid of selectable category cards (3-col desktop, 2-col mobile):

  👟 Sneakers & Footwear    [sneakers]
  👕 Streetwear & Apparel   [streetwear]
  💎 Luxury Fashion         [luxury_fashion]
  ⌚ Watches & Horology     [watches]
  🎨 Art & Prints           [art]
  🎮 Electronics & Tech     [electronics]
  🎵 Concerts & Events      [concert_tickets]
  🏆 Sports Memorabilia     [sports_memorabilia]
  🃏 Trading Cards          [trading_cards]
  💿 Vinyl & Music          [vinyl_music]
  📚 Rare Books             [books_rare]
  ✦  Other                  [other]

  Click = select (red border + checkmark). Multi-select allowed.
  Long-press or second click = set as PRIMARY (gold star badge).
  One primary required to proceed.

STEP 3 — YOUR LIMITED EDITION STRATEGY
  Headline: 'How do you create exclusivity?'
  Multi-select checkboxes:
  ☐ Numbered/Serialised Editions
  ☐ Time-Limited Drops
  ☐ Quantity-Limited Drops
  ☐ Membership/Tier Access
  ☐ Collab Drops
  ☐ Seasonal / Annual Releases
  ☐ Physical + Digital Bundle
  ☐ Experience Drops (tickets, backstage, events)

  Save to brands.limited_edition_strategies as jsonb.
  CTA: 'Launch My Brand' (red, full-width)

ON SUBMIT
  1. Create auth user
  2. Insert into brands
  3. Insert into brand_subdomains with the slug
  4. Insert brand_categories (primary first)
  5. Create 3 default customer tiers:
     Tier 1 'Follower'  level=1 color='888888' no min requirements
     Tier 2 'Insider'   level=2 color='C0A060' min_purchases=2 OR min_spend=15000
     Tier 3 'Legend'    level=3 color='FFD700' min_purchases=5 OR min_spend=50000
  6. Update profile.role = 'brand'
  7. Redirect → /brand-dashboard with welcome modal
